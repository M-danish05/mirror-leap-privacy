Privacy Policy

App: Mirror Leap
Last updated: October 19, 2026

Short version: Mirror Leap is a fully offline game. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Mirror Leap" mobile application ("the App") handles information. By installing or using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect, request, or transmit:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data.
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or other sensitive device data.
  • Analytics, usage telemetry, or crash reports.
  • Any form of personally identifiable information.

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following information only on your device, using standard local storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores, completion times).
  • Collected in-game items and achievement progress.
  • User preferences (vibration, particles, reduce motion settings).

This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage from your device settings.

4. Internet Access

The App is fully offline. It does not require an internet connection to play, and it does not communicate with any external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party advertising networks, analytics tools, or social media integrations. No third parties receive any data from the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Since no personal information is collected, children can safely use the App without any privacy risk.

7. Permissions

The App does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or SMS. The vibration feature uses the standard device vibrator for in-game feedback only and can be disabled from the in-app Settings screen.

8. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no data to be compromised. Local game progress is stored using the device's standard application storage, which is protected by the operating system.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
